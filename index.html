The founders

The founders of Navitech are professionals in the China Financial Technology Sector with over 30 years experience, working for global and local MNCs such as NCR, Wincor Nixdorf, and Asino-Wincor.

During this period, we have undertaken various large-scale software projects, such as the Shanghai Pudong Development Bank Multivendor software development (2019), Wincor Nixdorf CMDV4 WOSA/XFS development (2006), and ICBC Multivendor software development on Wincor/NCR/Diebold ATM/CRM (2005).
